# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**March 15, 2026**

## World Consumer Rights Day

This holiday commemorates the speech given by US President John Kennedy in 1962. He then formulated four basic consumer rights: to information, choice, security, and representation. In Poland, World Consumer Day has been officially celebrated since 2000, and in the world since 1983.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-praw-konsumenta)

---

## European Consumer Day

It is the European equivalent of World Consumer Day, celebrated on the same day. The event aims to make consumers aware of their rights. As part of the holiday, the European Union organizes conferences on the most important issues affecting consumers from all Member States.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-konsumenta)

## Bakers and Confectioners' Day

Professional confectioners and bakers celebrate their holiday on March 15, because on this day they commemorate their patron saint - Saint. Klemens. Thanks to them, we can eat tasty cakes and rolls. Today, maybe it's worth baking something yourself?

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-piekarzy-i-cukiernikow)
