# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**January 26, 2026**

## World Customs Day

This holiday has been celebrated since 1983. Its purpose is to commemorate the founding of the World Customs Organization - Customs Cooperation Council, the inaugural session of which took place in Brussels on January 26, 1953. Poland joined the celebration of World Customs Day in 1992.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-celnictwa)

## Day of Dialogue with Islam

The world is already constructed in such a way that people profess different religions. However, we all pray to the same God and this should be the key to mutual acceptance. The Day of Dialogue with Islam, celebrated in the Catholic Church in Poland since 2001, aims to build understanding with the followers of Islam.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-dialogu-z-islamem)

---

## Transplant Day

The holiday commemorates the first successful kidney transplant in our country. It took place on January 26, 1966 at the 1st Surgical Clinic of the Medical Academy in Warsaw. The aim of Transplant Day is also to promote transplants from deceased persons.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-transplantacji)

---

## Day of the Other Half

The period of falling in love is one big chemical reaction for our brain, thanks to which we are joyful and look at the world through rose-colored glasses. On Second Half Day, it is worth appreciating your partner and doing something nice, e.g. buying a gift or organizing a romantic dinner.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-drugiej-polowki)

---

## Australia Day

The land of kangaroos and emus has its own day in the calendar. Australia Day draws attention to a continent rich in endemic fauna and flora. During this time, you can look for interesting facts about this region, and one of them is the fact that the highest peak is Mount Kościuszko.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-australii)
