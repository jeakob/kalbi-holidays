## Cryptology Day

Cryptology Day in 2007 was adopted by the University of Adam Mickiewicz in Poznań on the occasion of the 75th anniversary of breaking the code of the German Enigma coding machine. This significant feat was achieved on December 14, 1932 by three Polish mathematicians and cryptologists, graduates of the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences of Adam Mickiewicz University: Marian Rejewski, Jerzy Różycki and Henryk Zygalski.
