# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**January 28, 2026**

## European Data Protection Day

In today's computerized world, we are increasingly exposed to the leakage of our personal data. If they get into the wrong hands, we could be in really big trouble. On European Data Protection Day, it is worth learning how to protect our ID.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-ochrony-danych-osobowych)

## International Day of Mobilization against Nuclear War

As the name suggests, on this day each of us should mobilize against Nuclear War. If this happens, the world as we know it will never be the same again.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-mobilizacji-przeciwko-wojnie-nuklearnej)

---

## Pipes and Pipes Day

Pipes and pipes belong to a group of folk instruments that used to have the power to summon spring. On the occasion of the holiday, which falls in the middle of winter, it is worth trying to play a melody - maybe the warm days will come earlier?

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-piszczalek-i-fujarek)

---

## International LEGO Day

Lego bricks are for boys what Barbie dolls are for girls. The iconic toy has a huge impact on children's development and is celebrated on International Lego Day. On this day, even adult men can easily assemble the Millennium Falcon from blocks.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-lego)
