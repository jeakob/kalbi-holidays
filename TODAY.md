# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**May 23, 2026**

## Justice Day

This holiday is celebrated in honor of Giovanni Falcone, an Italian investigating judge who uncompromisingly fought the Sicilian mafia. On May 23, 1992, Falcone, his wife and the police officers protecting them were killed in a bomb attack. Justice Day was initiated by the European Association MEDEL "Judges and Prosecutors for Democracy and Freedom", in Poland it is coordinated by the Association of Polish Judges "Iustitia"

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-wymiaru-sprawiedliwosci)

## Father-in-law's Day

We celebrate Father-in-law's Day on the Saturday closest to May 22. This day is intended to show special gratitude and thanks to the fathers of our wives and husbands.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-tescia)

## Forwarder's Day

A freight forwarder is a person who organizes the transport of goods. He chooses the best driver, takes care of all the formalities and ensures that the shipment reaches its destination safely. It is thanks to them that, among others, stocked store shelves. Today we wish them every success!

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-spedytora)

## World Turtle Day

Although these animals are known for their longevity, they cannot survive without the help of humans. World Tortoise Day, initiated in 2000 by American Tortoise Rescue, can contribute to their protection.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-zolwia)

## International Day to End Obstetric Fistula

Obstetric fistula is a gynecological injury that occurs during difficult deliveries. On the International Day for the End of Obstetric Fistula, people talk loudly about the fact that women in poor countries live with this injury because they cannot afford expensive surgeries. This situation should change, which is why this issue is being publicized.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-konca-przetoki-polozniczej)
