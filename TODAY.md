## Pi Approximation Day

It is no coincidence that we celebrate Pi Approximation Day on July 22. 22/7 - a person with a mathematical flair immediately knows that by approximating it in a fraction we will get 3.14, which is the value of Pi. This holiday can be celebrated both by solving tasks and by eating pizza or cake. This is related to the pronunciation in English, where "Pi" and "Pie" sound almost identical. That's why apple pies and pizzas are very popular on this day in America. And in our country you can also drink beer :)

## European Day of Support for Victims of Hate Crime

It is a holiday that not only commemorates the victims of hatred and the tragic events of July 22 in various years (liquidation of the Warsaw Ghetto by the Germans, the massacre on the island of Utøya and the bombing in Oslo), but also aims to eliminate sick ideologies and racism from everyday life.

## World Brain Day

World Brain Day is an opportunity to draw attention to the fact that people should take care of this organ because it is crucial in our body. A proper diet, exercise and drinking water will certainly have a positive impact on brain function.
