## Interlingua Day

The initiative of this holiday is to promote Interlingua, an artificial auxiliary language. It uses words that sound similar in the most popular languages ​​in the world. The date of celebration on January 15 commemorates the publication of Interlingua by the International Auxiliary Language Association in 1951.

## Wikipedia Day

## Bagel Day

Bagels are often confused with obwarzanki. They were most likely created in Krakow in the first half of the 17th century. Today they are less popular, but they have their own Bagel Day, on which bakeries specially prepare this delicious bread in salty and sweet versions.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-bajgla)
