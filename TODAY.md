**May 25, 2026**

## World Football Day

World Football Day was proclaimed by the United Nations in 2024, commemorating the 100th anniversary of the first global football tournament from the 1924 Paris Olympics. It is the first international day dedicated to a specific sport that uses the popularity of football to build peace and implement the Sustainable Development Goals. The UN has recognized football as a "universal language" capable of breaking down cultural and social barriers around the world.

## International Day of the Missing Child

The International Day of the Missing Child aims to draw attention to the problem of missing children and young people. It was established in 1983 by the then President of the United States, Ronald Reagan, in memory of 6-year-old Etan Patz. The boy disappeared on May 25, 1979 in the SoHo district of New York and was never found. The symbol of memory of the missing is the forget-me-not flower.

## Princess Day

Princess Day is a magical adventure that does not end when the day ends. It is a time when dreams, uniqueness and inner strength that lie in each of us are celebrated. Even though the royal costumes are put aside, the lessons and joy that accompanied us on that day become a part of us and remind us that we are unique, brave and full of love. Princess Day is not just a holiday, it is a reminder that each of us has an extraordinary ability to transform the world for the better.

## Stampmaker's Day

Stampsmith's Day is a celebration of people whose profession is currently little known. Nowadays, stamps are rarely used, although it was once a popular way, for example, to mark shipments. Today, stamps can still be found on official documents.

## Towel Day

This holiday is a tribute to science fiction writer Douglas Adams. His fans wear towels on this day, which is a reference to the British artist's famous work "The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy".

## Veterans Day

People who fought in war deserve respect. Veterans Day is intended to draw attention to the problems these people struggle with. Post-traumatic stress syndrome is a common condition of veterans that excludes them from social life. So is disability related to mission injuries.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-weterana)

## African Liberation Day

The holiday commemorates the First Conference of Independent African Countries and the Founding Act of the African Union. Thanks to them, the countries of this continent freed themselves from foreign domination and exploitation.
