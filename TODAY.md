# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**April 23, 2026**

## World Table Tennis Day

Discover the celebration that turned a simple sport into a global movement for diversity and inclusion! World Table Tennis Day is a unique initiative that since 2015 has used the universal language of ping-pong to promote equality and accessibility, inviting everyone - from amateurs to professionals - to celebrate a sport that truly belongs to everyone.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-tenisa-stolowego)

## World Book and Copyright Day

World Book and Copyright Day has been regularly celebrated on April 23 since 1995. The aim of this day is to promote reading, but also to protect intellectual property through copyright.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-ksiazki-i-praw-autorskich)

## Geographer's Day

Without geographical discoveries, the world would probably look different. On April 23, we commemorate those who devoted their lives to geography.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-geografa)

## International Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva (FOP) Awareness Day

This rare disease involves the formation of additional bones outside the natural skeleton, which immobilizes the body in one position and is associated with severe pain. International Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva Awareness Day was established by the International FOP Association to commemorate the discovery of the ACVR1 gene mutation responsible for the occurrence of progressive myositis ossificans.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-swiadomosci-fibrodysplasia-ossificans-progressiva-fop)

## Spanish Language Day

Spanish is the second most widely spoken language on the planet. Spanish Language Day aims to highlight its global importance and, of course, encourage learning.

## English Language Day

Approximately 20% of people in the world currently speak English. English Language Day draws attention to the fact that knowing foreign languages ​​makes it easier to travel, make new contacts and find a job. On this day, young people are encouraged to spend more time learning the language of Shakespeare.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-jezyka-angielskiego)
