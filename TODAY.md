## International Youth Solidarity Day

Although it is a celebration of youth, its "internship" definitely falls into the senior age category. It was celebrated for the first time in 1957 at the request of the World Federation of Democratic Youth.

## International Day Against Vivisection

Vivsection is a surgical procedure performed on a living animal. Is it worth inflicting suffering on another creature, even if it brings benefits?
