## Public Service Day

Public Service Day aims to highlight the contribution of public service to the process of development, democracy and world peace. At the same time, young people are encouraged to start a career in the public sector. The first celebration took place in 2003.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-sluzby-publicznej)

## International Widows Day

Today is a day to support women who have lost their husbands. This is not only the pain after the death of a loved one, but also the reason why they are left without the basic necessities of life. The holiday was established in December 2010.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-wdow)

## International Women in Engineering Day

International Women in Engineering Day is an international awareness campaign designed to raise the profile of women in engineering and focus attention on the incredible career opportunities available to girls in this exciting industry.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-kobiet-w-inzynierii)

## Olympic Day

Olympic Day is organized by most of the 206 member states of the International Olympic Committee. The aim of this global undertaking - in addition to commemorating the birth of the modern Olympic Games - is to promote sport, a healthy lifestyle, the Olympic idea and the principle of fair play.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-olimpijski)

---

## National Prostate Cancer Awareness Day

Men over 50 are at risk of prostate problems. The National Prostate Cancer Awareness Day involves organizing campaigns and events to reach men at risk and encourage them to visit a doctor for check-ups.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/ogolnopolski-dzien-swiadomosci-raka-prostaty)
