# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**April 05, 2026**

## Courtesy at the Wheel Day

Anyone who drives a car has probably cursed or honked at another driver for no reason more than once. Courtesy Behind the Wheel Day is a good opportunity to change that.

## Forester and Woodcutter's Day

Man owes a lot to trees: oxygen, fuel, fruit, paper. Not to mention how much pleasure a walk in the forest is. On Forester and Woodkeeper's Day, it is worth thinking about those who care for trees

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-lesnika-i-drzewiarza)

---

## Carmel Day

Liquid golden sugar used to decorate cakes and desserts has a special place in the calendar, i.e. Caramel Day. You can celebrate from morning to evening by eating cookies layered with this sweet delicacy or ice cream with caramel topping. However, it is worth eating in moderation, because you can overeat it!

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-karmelu)
