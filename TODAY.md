# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**March 14, 2026**

## Pi Day

It is a holiday that is intended to promote mathematics and science. To make things more interesting, we celebrate it on March 14, which, according to the American date format (3.14), corresponds to the value of the number Pi. The first event took place in 1988 in San Francisco, initiated by Larry Shaw. Meetings and events related to the holiday are organized mainly in the United States.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-liczby-pi)

## White Day

This holiday comes from Japan. On March 14, men give gifts to women. It is like a revenge for Valentine's Day, because according to the custom in the Land of the Rising Sun, ladies give gifts on February 14.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/bialy-dzien)

---

## International Day Against Dams

The holiday coordinated by the International Rivers Network from the United States aims to make people aware of the negative effects of building large dams. In our country, the organizer of this event is the Gaja club.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-sprzeciwu-wobec-tam)

---

## Butterfly Day

Beautiful and colorful insects that evoke only positive emotions have their own time in the calendar, i.e. Butterfly Day. This is the moment to pay attention to the need, among others, to: taking care of air quality, which affects the survival of the population of these wonderful creatures.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-motyli)
