## World Amateur Radio Day

World Amateur Radio Day (WARD) is celebrated on April 18 - this day marks the anniversary of the establishment of the international amateur radio organization. In 1925, communities from various national organizations united in the International Amateur Radio Union, which gave rise to amateur radio without borders.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-krotkofalowca)

## European Patients' Rights Day

European Patients' Rights Day is celebrated in all European Union countries. Its aim is to recall the 14 rights contained in the European Charter of Patients' Rights, which guarantee a "high level of protection of human health".

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-praw-pacjenta)

## Coma Patient Day

Coma Patient Day is celebrated thanks to Ewa Błaszczyk's "Akogo?" foundation, which submitted such an initiative to the Parliamentary Health Committee. The aim of the holiday is to draw attention to the problems of patients who cannot take care of it themselves. .

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-pacjenta-w-spiaczce)
