## World Kociewie Day

Kociewie is a unique ethno-cultural region in Gdańsk Pomerania. The International Kociewie Day was announced in 2007 on the initiative of members of the Kociewie Culture Association in Starogard Gd. and the Kociewie Local Tourist Organization in Tczew. Its aim is to promote this region, and the date of the celebration refers to the first note about Kociewie from February 10, 1807.

## Mincer's Day

A mint worker is a mint worker who produces coins. The holiday commemorates the establishment of the first mint in Poland on February 10, 1766.

## World Dry Legumes Day

Legumes contain many positive macro and microelements. In addition, they boast a high protein content. World Dry Pulses Day was established to popularize legumes in the diet due to their wonderful health-promoting properties.
