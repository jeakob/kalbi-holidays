# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**May 12, 2026**

## International Nurses Day

International Nurses Day is celebrated on May 12 and aims to thank all nurses for their daily mission of helping all sick people.
Dear nurses - if it weren't for them, the stay in the hospital would be completely unbearable.

## European Melanoma Day

In the case of melanoma, like many other diseases, early diagnosis allows for cure. European Melanoma Day is a good opportunity to take advantage of free tests.

## European Stroke Prevention Day

Stroke is a common cause of death. Its causes are believed to be an improper diet and lack of activity. According to experts, the fact that it may have a genetic basis is also important.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-profilaktyki-udarowej)

## Chronic Fatigue Syndrome and Immune Disorders Day

This holiday has been celebrated since 1993, its originator is Tom Hennessy - the founder of a foundation dedicated to refuting stereotypes regarding chronic immunological and neurological diseases. The day commemorates the birth anniversary of Florence Nightingale, the founder of modern nursing, who was diagnosed with this disease.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-syndromu-chronicznego-zmeczenia-i-zaburzen-immunologicznych)

## International Limerick Day

We celebrate Limerick Day, i.e. short, humorous songs, on May 12. It is the birthday of Edward Lear, who popularized limericks in the mid-19th century. Today, it is worth improving your mood by reading English or Polish writers - e.g. Tuwim or Szymborska.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-limerykow)

---

## Fibromyalgia Awareness Day

This holiday aims to make society aware of what Fibromyalgia is. This disease characterized by chronic muscle pain is extremely nasty because it is difficult to pinpoint its causes, it is accompanied by severe pain, and the symptoms persist despite treatment.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-wiedzy-o-fibromialgii)
