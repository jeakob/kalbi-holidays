**May 31, 2026**

## World No Tobacco Day

In Poland, the percentage of smokers dropped from 42% to 24% - one of the greatest public health successes in the history of our country. It all started with a simple WHO resolution in 1987, which triggered an avalanche of changes: from smoking bans in public places to revolutionary therapies to help quit smoking. Today, anyone who quits smoking can regain almost 100% of their health - even after decades of addiction.

## White Stork Day

This holiday was born out of concern for a species that has become a living barometer of the state of Polish nature. The white stork is not only a beautiful bird, but a true indicator of the health of our ecosystems - where storks live, nature thrives. The day was established to remind us that protecting these majestic birds means protecting the entire landscape of the Polish countryside.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-bociana-bialego)

## Food Industry Worker's Day

In the Polish People's Republic calendar, it was a movable date - the last Sunday of May, entered in the Monitor Polski from 1973. Today it is always celebrated on the same day, because this is established by industry practice, not a new legal act. This is proof that a professional tradition can outlive the system that created it.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-pracownika-przemyslu-spozywczego)
