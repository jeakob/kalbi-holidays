## Antiquities Day

Antiques Day is a holiday reminding us of the value of items from the past in the era of rapid consumption. This is a great opportunity to search the attic, visit an antique fair or organize an exhibition of family souvenirs. Old items not only have sentimental and historical value, but also create bridges between generations.
