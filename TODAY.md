# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**May 24, 2026**

## Brothers' Day

You never lose brothers - this popular saying will always be true. However, you need to take care of your siblings, and Brothers' Day, which falls on May 24, effectively reminds us of this.

## Cyrillic Day

The Cyrillic alphabet is an alphabetic script used to write many Slavic languages. A challenge for today's holiday? Let's write our name in Cyrillic!

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-cyrylicy)

## European National Parks Day

This holiday was invented in honor of the first national park in Europe, which is Sarek in Sweden. Since May 24, 1909, when it was established, over 250 parks have been created on our continent, including over 20 in Poland.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-parkow-narodowych)

---

## Snail Day

The snail has many nutritional properties and has been considered an aphrodisiac for hundreds of years. No wonder the French love to eat them. It's time for Poles to like them too.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-slimaka)

---

## Special Forces Day

Special Forces is an elite group of soldiers that takes part in the most difficult military operations. May 24 is the anniversary of the 2007 act under which Special Forces obtained the status of a separate type of Armed Forces.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swieto-wojsk-specjalnych)
