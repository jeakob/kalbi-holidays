# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**May 17, 2026**

## World Hypertension Day

In Poland, over 10 million people have hypertension - and many of them do not know about it. The World Hypertension League created this day precisely for this purpose: not to remind people about the disease, but to encourage action. Measuring your blood pressure is the first step that millions miss.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-nadcisnienia-tetniczego)

## International Telecommunications Day

This holiday commemorated the founding of the International Telecommunication Union (May 17, 1865). In 2006, it was renamed World Telecommunications and Information Society Day.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-telekomunikacji)

---

## World Information Society Day

This holiday was established in 2006. The United Nations replaced International Telecommunications Day with it. On the occasion of this event, various events dedicated to new technologies are organized around the world.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-spoleczenstwa-informacyjnego)

## International Day Against Homophobia, Transphobia and Biphobia

This holiday commemorates the decision of the World Health Organization on May 17, 1990, thanks to which homosexuality was removed from the International Classification of Diseases.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-przeciw-homofobii-transfobii-i-bifobii)

## International Children's Helplines Day

The initiator of the holiday is an organization that brings together helplines for children from all over the world. The aim of the campaign is to draw attention to this form of help for the youngest.

## World Baking Day
