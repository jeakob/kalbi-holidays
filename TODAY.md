# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**April 04, 2026**

## Carrot Day

Carrot Day (International Carrot Day) falls on April 4 - a holiday intended to encourage the consumption of this tasty and healthy vegetable. The first celebrations took place in 2003.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-marchewki)

## International Pillow Fight Day

The biggest advantage of this holiday is that no one will get hurt during the fight. Instead of blood, there's just feathers everywhere...

## International Mine Awareness and Mine Prevention Day

Mines pose a huge threat to humans - contact with them may result in disability at best. Conducting anti-mine campaigns aims to draw attention to a problem that is very serious in some countries - it threatens life, but also hinders economic progress.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-wiedzy-o-minach-i-dzialan-zapobiegajacych-minom)

## World Homeless Animals Day

Not only people need the warmth of home. Animals also become attached to places, they feel the lack of shelter, hunger and cold. On this day, let's think about homeless animals: let's take food to a shelter, let's adopt a dog from the street. The reward is boundless gratitude... »

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-bezdomnych-zwierzat)

Soldiers keep us safe, but does anyone watch over them? Absolutely - the Military Health Service, which celebrates its holiday on April 4

## World Rat Day

Rats are not very likable creatures. They arouse disgust and fear, they chew through cables and destroy crops. But they can also be useful, and what's more, they have their supporters. World Rat Day is the best proof of this.
