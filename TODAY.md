# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**April 07, 2026**

## International Dollhouse Day

International Dollhouse Day is a unique holiday that promotes knowledge about historical dollhouses as material evidence of the past. The holiday encourages creativity and spending time together, offering competitions, inspiration for creating miniatures and family integration. Discover the passion that connects generations and create a common souvenir with hidden stories and meanings.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-domkow-lalek)
