## European Day of e-Civic Engagement

This holiday aims to make Europeans aware of the fact that they can contact representatives of institutions in Poland and on the Old Continent electronically. Both submit petitions, applications or create a local budget.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/europejski-dzien-e-aktywnosci-obywatelskiej)

## World Password Day

Passwords are common in today's world - this is how we secure our online, telephone, e-mail or Facebook accounts. On World Password Day, it is worth learning about the basic rules for creating secure passwords.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-hasla)
