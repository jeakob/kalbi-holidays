# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**April 25, 2026**

## Secretary's Day

Secretaries who do hard work, have to keep track of deadlines, arrange meetings and do many other things are often underestimated. Secretary's Day is a great opportunity for the boss to appreciate his employee and reward her commitment, e.g. with a raise or at least a kind word.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-sekretarki)

---

## Son-in-law's Day

Son-in-law's Day is celebrated on the last Saturday of April to commemorate the day when, on the last Saturday of April, a programmer became a son-in-law.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-ziecia)

## International Noise Awareness Day

The aim of the holiday is to draw attention to the common occurrence of noise in people's lives and its negative impact on health. The holiday was established by the League of the Hearing Impaired

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-swiadomosci-zagrozenia-halasem)

---

## Parental Alienation Awareness Day

The aim of this campaign is to raise awareness of society about parental alienation, i.e. disorders in the situation of parents separating in the relationship between a child and the other parent. The celebrations have been held in our country since 2008 with the participation of the Warning Committee against Parental Separation.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-swiadomosci-alienacji-rodzicielskiej)

---

## World Malaria Day

The aim of celebrating World Malaria Day is to increase public awareness and reduce the effects of this tropical parasitic disease.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-malarii)

---

## DNA Day

We celebrate DNA Day on April 25 in honor of two events. First, on this day in 1953, it was announced how DNA is constructed, and 50 years later the National Institute of Health in the USA issued an announcement that the final stage of the Human Genome Project was coming. Initially, it was supposed to be a one-time event, but it quickly turned into a celebration of molecular biologists.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-dna)

---

## World Penguin Day

It is a holiday established in honor of an interesting discovery. Scientists observed that for 7 years in a row, the Adelie penguin colony, after spending months at sea, returned to Antarctica to its breeding grounds - each time on April 25. On this day, scientists are waiting for these nice animals.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-pingwina)

---

## World Veterinarians Day

When our beloved dog or kitten gets sick, their help is invaluable. Thanks to World Veterinarians' Day, we can realize the role they play in society.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-lekarzy-weterynarii)

## Tai Chi Day

On Tai Chi day, it is worth trying these exercises originating from Ancient China. Not only do they relax, but they add energy and can be useful when someone attacks us.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-tai-chi)
