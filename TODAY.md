## World Plant Milk Day

World Plant Milk Day is an initiative to raise awareness of alternatives to cow's milk. The event promotes the diversity of plant-based milks, highlighting their potential benefits for health, the environment and animal welfare, while encouraging the exploration of new flavors and culinary uses.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-mleka-roslinnego)

## International Day Commemorating the Victims of Acts of Violence Based on Religion or Belief

The International Day Commemorating the Victims of Acts of Violence Based on Religion or Belief is a moment when the memory of all people who died because they did not deny their faith is honored. Religious persecution is still widespread in some regions of the world, and on this day, special attention is paid to this problem.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-upamietniajacy-ofiary-aktow-przemocy-ze-wzgledu-na-religie-lub-wyznanie)
