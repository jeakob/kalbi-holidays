## International Day of Remembrance for the Victims of the Holocaust

The holiday was established on November 1, 2005 to honor the memory of victims of Jewish origin murdered by Nazi Germany during World War II. In Poland, the main celebrations take place in Auschwitz-Birkenau and at the Warsaw Ghetto Heroes' Monument.
