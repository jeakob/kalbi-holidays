## Navy Day

Poland, as a maritime nation, has been building a special bond with the Baltic Sea for centuries, and Navy Day is the most important expression of this relationship. Sailors, shipyard workers, port workers and fishermen celebrate together, emphasizing that maritime safety is the concern of the entire society. The celebrations show pride in Polish sailing traditions and the modern fleet that guards our maritime borders.

## National Remembrance Day of Poznań June 1956

A delegation of workers returned from Warsaw empty-handed - the minister made promises and then took them back. The next morning the strike siren sounded. Among the hundred thousand protesters was 13-year-old Romek Strzałkowski - the youngest of the victims who died near the Poznań Castle before the tanks arrived.

## Brother-in-law's Day

Who exactly is a brother-in-law and why does he deserve his own holiday? This is a man who has to endure family jokes, participate in endless discussions, and pretend to understand all the internal family codes. Brother-in-law's Day is a moment to appreciate his patience and effort in building relationships with his "new family".
