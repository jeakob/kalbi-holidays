## National Remembrance Day of the "Cursed Soldiers"

The cursed soldiers were properly appreciated for fighting for Poland only several dozen years after the end of World War II. On March 1, we celebrate the National Remembrance Day of the "Cursed Soldiers", which commemorates the soldiers of the anti-communist underground in Poland.

## Zero for Discrimination

The main message of Non-Discrimination Day is an encouragement to promote and celebrate diversity and reject all forms of discrimination. The day aims to raise awareness of the problem of discrimination and remind people that any form of discrimination is a violation of human rights and an obstacle to achieving equality and social justice. Non-Discrimination Day aims to inspire people to take action to help build a more inclusive and equal world where every person is treated with respect and dignity.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/zero-dla-dyskryminacji)

## World Seagrass Day

Covering just 0.1% of the ocean floor, seagrasses store as much as 18% of the world's ocean carbon, making them a key ally in the fight against climate change. These underwater meadows are home to thousands of marine species and provide natural coastal protection, yet we lose an area the size of a football field of these valuable ecosystems every 30 minutes.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-trawy-morskiej)

## International Day against Nuclear Weapons

This is an initiative that opposes nuclear weapons, but also commemorates the victims of nuclear weapons. On March 1, 1954, the American army carried out Operation Castle Bravo, which involved detonating an experimental hydrogen bomb. As a result of the failed attempt, approximately 300 people were contaminated.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-walki-przeciwko-zbrojeniom-atomowym)

## World Civil Defense Day

The holiday was established by the International Civil Defense Organization (ICDO). It commemorates the entry into force of the ICDO constitution as an intergovernmental organization in 1972. The Day's celebration aims to emphasize the importance of civil defense and raise public awareness of self-defense, as well as to appreciate all services responsible for combating natural disasters.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swiatowy-dzien-obrony-cywilnej)
