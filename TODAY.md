## International No Plastic Bags Day

Plastic is the material that takes the longest to decompose. International No Plastic Bags Day is an action aimed at reducing the use of popular plastic bags, which are disposable and after shopping they immediately end up in the trash, and then litter the oceans.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-bez-toreb-foliowych)

## Aunt and Uncle's Day
