# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**June 29, 2026**

## Fisherman's Day

A procession of decorated boats, flares on the water, the smell of smoked fish and the singing of shanties - this is how fishing ports celebrate people of the sea. Fisherman's Day is a tribute to one of the oldest and most beautiful professions
  seaside traditions. A combination of spirituality, professional pride and family fun that brings generations together.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-rybaka)

## WOPR Rescuer's Day

The holiday, established almost 100 years ago, today celebrates a profession that has a dramatic lack of people willing to work. Rescuers earn PLN 6-7,000 gross for a month's work without days off, and human lives depend on their seconds of inattention. It's a story about how society values ​​heroes in theory but doesn't support them in practice.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-ratownika-wopr)

## Military Economic Troops Day

During exercises, a soldier receives fuel, ammunition and food - and rarely wonders who organizes it. The answer is Military Economic Troops: structures established by the decision of the Ministry of National Defense, which took over all logistics, finances and administration from the commanders. Without them, a modern army would come to a standstill.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/swieto-wojskowych-oddzialow-gospodarczych)

## International Tropics Day

Every year on June 29, the world turns its attention to a region that makes up 40% of the Earth's surface and is home to 80% of the world's biodiversity. The tropics are an area where over a billion people struggle with health and economic challenges, but also a place of spectacular successes - in the last decade, 600 million people stopped requiring medical interventions against neglected tropical diseases.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-tropikow)
