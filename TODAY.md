## Bridge Worker's Day

Although the first bridges in Poland were built hundreds of years ago, it was only on April 25, 2013 that the Bridge Workers' Day was established. This happened due to the Resolution of the Management Board of the Association of Bridge Workers of the Republic of Poland. We wish all industry representatives our best wishes.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-mostowca)
