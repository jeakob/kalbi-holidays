## World Chocolate Day

Few products are as powerful and as addictive as chocolate. Considering such a rich history, it may be surprising that its gourmets only came up with the idea of ​​establishing an official holiday in 2009. The date of July 7 refers to the introduction of chocolate to Europe, although it was already known to the Mayans. Today, I don't think any sweet tooth needs to be encouraged to celebrate.
