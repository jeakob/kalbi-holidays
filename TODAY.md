## Day Against Internet Censorship

The initiator of this holiday is the Reporters Without Borders organization, and its aim is to fight against the restriction of freedom of speech on the Internet. In authoritarian countries, the Internet is the only independent source of information, although governments try to fight it by all means. As part of the celebration, an award is given for special contributions to promoting freedom of expression online.
