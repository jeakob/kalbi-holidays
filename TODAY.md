## Village Head's Day

The village head is one of the oldest social functions in Poland, dating back to the Middle Ages. March 11 is the holiday of people considered to be the leaders of Polish villages - Village Head's Day.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-soltysa)

## Zoo and Aquarium Mission Day

Did you know that modern zoos are not only places for exhibiting exotic animals, but real centers for the protection of endangered species? Mission Day of Zoos and Aquariums, initiated by the Warsaw Zoo, emphasizes the key role of these institutions in saving the biodiversity of our planet. Thanks to breeding and reintroduction programs, zoos have restored species such as Przewalski's horse and peregrine falcon to nature - see how you can support their mission!

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-misji-ogrodow-zoologicznych-i-akwariow)
