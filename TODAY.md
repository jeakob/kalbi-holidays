# 🎉 Today's Unusual Holidays

**February 21, 2026**

## International Mother Language Day

The aim of International Mother Language Day is to protect linguistic diversity as a cultural heritage. The holiday falls on February 21 and was adopted by UNESCO in 1999.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-jezyka-ojczystego)

---

## Day of the Struggle against the Colonial Regime

History is known for brutal attacks on the inhabitants of colonies by superior countries. The Day for the Struggle against the Colonial Regime draws attention to the fact that even today, indigenous people in some regions feel threatened and such situations need to be prevented.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/dzien-walki-z-rezimem-kolonialnym)

---

## International Tourist Guide Day

Anyone who likes to explore interesting places has a good opportunity to do so today. On the occasion of Tourist Guides Day, in many cities you can take part in free sightseeing with guides.

[Learn more](https://www.kalbi.pl/miedzynarodowy-dzien-przewodnika-turystycznego)
