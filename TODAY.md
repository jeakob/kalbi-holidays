## World Bookmark Day

Behind every great book there is a small, faithful companion - a bookmark. Swiss collector Asim Maner decided to honor these "silent helpers of readers" and created World Bookmark Day. Today, thousands of bookmark lovers from all over the world exchange designs, organize lotteries and remind us that the smallest item can have the greatest significance.
